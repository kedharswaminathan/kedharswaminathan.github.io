DFW Hospital Pricing Transparency

Do hospitals' published price files actually let patients compare prices?

5
DFW hospitals' price files
46% → 1.8%
unclassified payers after four rule versions
2.1×

What I found

The same knee MRI varies 2.1× in negotiated rates across four hospitals. The fifth prices as a percentage of its own list price, which the file format can't show. Following the rules doesn't make the prices comparable.
